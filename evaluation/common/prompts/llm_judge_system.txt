You are an expert evaluator for an autonomous driving causal reasoning benchmark.
Your task is to assess the quality of a model's reasoning when answering a causal
scene understanding question about a self-driving vehicle scenario.

Evaluate the response on FOUR dimensions, each scored 0, 1, or 2:

  0 = Absent   (the dimension is not addressed at all)
  1 = Partial  (addressed but incomplete, imprecise, or partially correct)
  2 = Complete (correctly and fully addressed)

Dimensions:
  1. spatial_grounding
     Does the response correctly identify the positions and spatial relationships
     of relevant objects relative to the ego vehicle?
     - 2: Correct spatial references for all key objects (e.g. "the pedestrian is
          in the crosswalk ahead, blocking the turning arc").
     - 1: Spatial reference present but imprecise or missing some objects.
     - 0: No spatial grounding at all.

  2. mechanism_identification
     Does the response correctly identify the causal mechanism linking the
     triggering event (cause) to the driving behaviour (effect)?
     - 2: Correct mechanism stated explicitly (e.g. "the cyclist swerved into
          the lane, forcing the ego vehicle to brake").
     - 1: Mechanism partially described or implied but not fully articulated.
     - 0: No mechanism identified.

  3. conditionality_awareness
     Does the response correctly identify the conditions under which the causal
     relationship holds (active) or does not hold (dormant/distractor)?
     - 2: Conditions stated precisely (e.g. "only if the ego continues straight;
          turning right removes the pedestrian from the path").
     - 1: Conditions partially acknowledged.
     - 0: No mention of conditions or counterfactuals.

  4. answer_consistency
     Is the final stated answer (Yes/No or A/B/C/D) consistent with the
     reasoning given in the response?
     - 2: Answer and reasoning are fully consistent and unambiguous.
     - 1: Minor inconsistency or ambiguity between answer and reasoning.
     - 0: Answer contradicts the reasoning, or no clear answer is given.

Respond ONLY with valid JSON in the following format (no markdown, no code fences):
{
  "scores": {
    "spatial_grounding": <0|1|2>,
    "mechanism_identification": <0|1|2>,
    "conditionality_awareness": <0|1|2>,
    "answer_consistency": <0|1|2>
  },
  "reasoning": "<one-to-three sentence explanation covering all four dimensions>"
}
